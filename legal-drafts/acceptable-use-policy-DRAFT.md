# Acceptable Use Policy

**Last updated:** [DATE — set on publish]

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") applies to everyone who uses Flow, and
is incorporated into our [Terms of Service] by reference. Flow gives you
real communication power — email, SMS, WhatsApp, voice calling, and AI
agents that can message or call people on your behalf. That power comes
with responsibility, both to the people you contact and to keeping Flow
usable for everyone else on the platform.

## 1. You are responsible for who you contact and why

Flow is a tool for managing relationships with people who have a
legitimate connection to your business — leads, prospects, customers, and
people who have interacted with your forms, website, or booking pages. You
are solely responsible for:

- Having a lawful basis and, where required, consent to contact each
  person by email, SMS, WhatsApp, or voice call;
- Complying with CAN-SPAM, TCPA, GDPR, and any other law that applies to
  your messages, calls, and campaigns, in every jurisdiction you operate
  in or contact people in;
- Honoring opt-out requests promptly. Flow processes STOP/START replies
  automatically for SMS and flips the relevant opt-out flag — you may not
  attempt to re-contact someone who has opted out through a channel other
  than the one they used to opt out, or otherwise work around this
  mechanism;
- Outbound voice campaigns specifically: Flow's compliance gate checks
  consent, calling windows, and frequency caps before every call, but this
  is a safety net, not a substitute for your own compliance obligations —
  you must have a lawful basis to call each number before you ever load it
  into a campaign.

## 2. Prohibited content and conduct

You may not use Flow to:

- Send spam, unsolicited bulk messages, or content to people who have not
  given you a basis to contact them;
- Send phishing, malware, or deceptive content, or impersonate any person
  or organization;
- Send content that is unlawful, defamatory, harassing, hateful,
  discriminatory, or that infringes someone else's intellectual property
  or privacy rights;
- Collect or import contact lists you do not have the right to use (for
  example, purchased or scraped lists without a lawful basis to contact
  those people);
- Use the AI Agents to impersonate a human where disclosure of automated/
  AI communication is legally required, or to deceive recipients about the
  nature of the interaction;
- Use Flow to facilitate illegal activity of any kind, including fraud,
  money laundering, or the sale of prohibited goods or services.

## 3. Platform integrity

You may not:

- Attempt to bypass, disable, or interfere with rate limits, the outbound
  voice compliance gate, opt-out handling, or any other safety or security
  control built into Flow;
- Attempt to gain unauthorized access to another workspace, sub-account,
  or user's data;
- Use automated means to scrape, extract, or bulk-export data from Flow
  outside the tools we provide for that purpose (for example, the CSV
  export feature);
- Probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of Flow's systems without our
  prior written permission;
- Use the Service to build a product that competes with Flow using data or
  access obtained through your account;
- Resell or provide access to Flow to a third party outside the normal
  agency/sub-account/member model built into the product.

## 4. AI Agents specifically

If you enable AI Agents (web chat, SMS, WhatsApp, voice, or outbound
voice), you are responsible for:

- Reviewing your agent's persona/prompt configuration so it does not
  produce misleading, harmful, or non-compliant content;
- Disclosing the use of an AI agent to the people you contact where
  required by law or platform policy of a connected channel (for example,
  WhatsApp's own policies);
- Not configuring an AI agent to make claims — medical, legal, financial,
  or otherwise — that require professional licensing or that you could not
  make yourself.

See our [Responsible AI Policy] for more on how these features work and
their limitations.

## 5. Enforcement

If we reasonably believe you've violated this AUP, we may, depending on
severity: warn you, suspend the specific feature involved (for example,
outbound messaging or voice calling) while preserving the rest of your
account, or suspend or terminate your account under our [Terms of
Service]. We will generally try to notify you and give you an opportunity
to correct the issue first, except where we believe immediate action is
necessary to prevent harm — for example, active abuse affecting other
users or third parties.
