# Security

**Last updated:** [DATE — set on publish]

How we protect your data on Flow.

## Infrastructure

Flow runs on Firebase and Google Cloud infrastructure. Your data — your
account, your contacts, your conversations, your configuration — is
stored in Firestore, Google's managed database, and benefits from Google
Cloud's underlying infrastructure security.

## Encryption

All traffic between you and Flow is encrypted in transit via HTTPS. Data
at rest is encrypted using Google Cloud's standard encryption for Firestore
and Firebase Storage.

## Workspace isolation

Flow is multi-tenant by design, and access is enforced at the data layer,
not just the UI: every workspace ("sub-account") is isolated by tenancy
rules in our Firestore security rules, so one workspace's data is never
readable by another workspace's users. Within a workspace, roles (admin
vs. collaborator) control what a given member can see and do.

## Authentication

Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication. We never store your
password directly — Firebase manages credential storage and hashing.
Sessions use signed, HttpOnly cookies that aren't accessible to page
scripts, reducing exposure to cross-site scripting attacks.

## Secrets & API keys

API keys you generate (for our public API) and sensitive configuration
(webhook signing secrets, third-party credentials you connect) are hashed
or encrypted before storage — we don't keep them in plain text. Outbound
webhooks are signed (HMAC-SHA256) so receivers can verify a payload
genuinely came from Flow. Public, single-use links generated by the
platform (for example, quote-approval links and unsubscribe links) use
signed, expiring tokens rather than guessable IDs.

## Payment data

We never handle your raw card details. Subscription billing and in-app
payment features are processed by Stripe, a PCI-compliant payment
processor — card numbers pass directly to Stripe and never touch our
servers.

## Access control for AI features

Certain AI-assistant actions that write data or take real-world effect
require explicit confirmation before they execute, rather than running
automatically — this is a deliberate design choice to keep a human in the
loop for consequential actions. See our Responsible AI Policy for more.

## What this page doesn't claim

In the interest of accuracy: Flow has not undergone a third-party security
audit, penetration test, or compliance certification (such as SOC 2) as of
this writing. We do not claim automatic or daily backups, point-in-time
recovery, or a specific disaster-recovery capability — if and when a
backup/recovery process is verified and in place, this page will be
updated to describe it accurately. This page describes the real technical
practices in place today, not a certification or capability we don't
hold.
